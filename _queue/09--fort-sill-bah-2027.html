How the yearly change works

Every year DoD surveys what rentals and utilities cost in each military housing area and sets new rates. They are announced in mid-December and start January 1. The 2026 rates came out on December 11, 2025.

The rates are built to cover most of the cost of renting in the area, not all of it. Service members are expected to cover a small share out of pocket. So when local rents rise, BAH tends to follow, and when they flatten, it can hold still or slip.

It does not always go up. Some years a rate for a given grade comes in a little under the year before, which is where rate protection comes in.

Rate protection

This is the part to understand before January.

  • If you are already here, your BAH cannot go down while you stay at Fort Sill at the same grade and dependency status. If the new rate is higher, you get the raise. If it is lower, you keep what you have.
  • If you arrive after January 1, you get the new year's rate, whichever way it moved.
  • Protection ends with a PCS, a reduction in pay grade, or a change in your dependency status.

That is why two people at the same rank in the same unit can draw slightly different BAH.

What a change means if you are buying

For a buyer, a change of twenty or thirty dollars a month in BAH moves the price it carries by a few thousand dollars. Every $100 a month is roughly $10,400 of house at 7.40%. The interest rate moves your buying power far more than a BAH adjustment does.

So if you are PCSing in early 2027, do not wait on the new table to start. Get pre-approved on the current rate, and when the new one posts, your lender updates one line. If you are already here and thinking about buying, rate protection means your number is not going down as long as your grade and dependents do not change.

If you are trying to decide between renting and buying on your allowance, the BAH calculator shows what your rate carries today, with Lawton's real tax and insurance in the payment.

Common questions

When do 2027 BAH rates come out?

DoD normally announces the next year's rates in mid-December. The 2026 rates were announced on December 11, 2025. New rates take effect January 1.

Can my BAH go down if the Fort Sill rate drops?

Not while you stay at Fort Sill at the same pay grade and dependency status. Individual rate protection means you are paid the higher of the new rate or what you were already receiving. You only move to a lower published rate after a PCS, a reduction in grade, or a change in dependents.

What BAH rate does a lender use if I am buying before I report?

With orders in hand, a lender can qualify you on the Fort Sill rate for your grade. If you report after January 1, that will be the 2027 rate once it is published.
