The honest part first

You have probably heard some things about Lawton. Somebody at your last duty station had an opinion, or you went down a thread about it at two in the morning.

Here is the straight answer. Lawton is a real city and it has real growing pains. It is not a suburb and it does not pretend to be. What it also has is a military community that has been here since 1869 and a support system built around it, which is not something you find in most places this size.

I am not going to tell you a neighborhood is safe or unsafe. That is not my call to make. What I will do is point you at the same sources for every town so you can read the numbers yourself. Resale and appreciation are different. That is market data, and I will show you the actual numbers on any addresses you are comparing.

Where to look up crime data for any town

  • FBI Crime Data Explorer, the reported data by agency
  • SafeWise Oklahoma rankings, cities sorted by reported crime per 1,000 residents
  • The police department for the specific city, which publishes local reports and answers questions from the public

Use the same three for every place you look at. Small towns have small numbers, so one incident can move a rate a long way. Look at more than one year before you conclude anything.

What to know before you pick a house

Getting on post

Fort Sill has six gates and they do not all run the same hours. Do not plan your commute around published gate hours. They change constantly. The My Army Post app carries current gate status. Any adult without a military ID needs a pass from the Visitor Welcome Center by Bentley Gate, so sort that out before family shows up to help you move in.

School districts

Five districts cover most of where Fort Sill families buy: Lawton, Elgin, Cache, Fletcher and Geronimo. The district follows the address, not the mailing city, and Medicine Park is split between two. I do not rate schools. I will tell you exactly which district serves any address you are looking at.

Which district serves which address →

The towns outside Lawton

A lot of families end up outside Lawton proper. The tradeoff is mostly price and drive time.

  • Elgin, north, off I-44. Newer houses, its own district.
  • Cache, west on Highway 62. Its own district.
  • Geronimo, south. A lot of new construction, still a small town.
  • Fletcher, northeast. Farther out, more land for the money.
  • Medicine Park, a cobblestone resort town at the edge of the Wichitas.

What your allowance shops

Before you fall for a house, get the number straight. Lawton property tax runs about 1.1% to 1.2% of value a year and insurance about $19.28 per $1,000 of coverage. Those two lines move a payment more than most people expect. If you draw VA disability pay, the funding fee is waived. At a 100% permanent rating, Oklahoma exempts a resident's homestead from property tax.

Buying before you get here

You do not have to wait until you report. With orders in hand a lender can qualify you on your Fort Sill allowance. I send listings and walk houses on video. Inspections and the appraisal happen while you are still in transit, and plenty of families close within a week or two of arriving.

Rent, buy, or keep it when you leave

There is a built-in rental market here. The post cycles people through constantly, so there is steady demand for houses near it. Plenty of families buy, live in it for a tour, and keep it as a rental when they PCS out. A single-family house rents for somewhere around $1,200 to $1,300 a month. Run it on the actual house before you count on it.

The thing nobody tells you about

The Wichita Mountains Wildlife Refuge sits about fifteen minutes from post. Roughly 59,000 acres of granite mountains, prairie, and lakes, with free-roaming bison, longhorn, and elk. A road runs to the top of Mount Scott when it is open. It is free. People stationed here for three years sometimes go twice. Go more than that.
