I am a real estate agent. I am not a tax preparer or a lawyer. These are the things military buyers ask about. For anything about your own return or your state of residence, the legal assistance office on post is free and it is the right place to ask.

State income tax

  • If Oklahoma is your legal residence: all of your active-duty pay is excluded from Oklahoma income tax. That includes Guard and Reserve pay.
  • If you are a resident of another state stationed here: Oklahoma does not tax your military pay. Your home state's rules still apply.
  • A side job is different. Money you earn from a civilian job in Oklahoma is Oklahoma income.
  • Spouses: a military spouse who is here because of the service member's orders can generally keep the same state of residence as the service member, and wages can be exempt from Oklahoma tax on that basis. Ask legal assistance or a tax preparer how to set that up.
  • Retirement pay: military retirement has been 100% excluded from Oklahoma income tax since 2022.

That last one is one reason people retire here.

Vehicle tags

Many service members keep their home-state registration while they are stationed here. A tag agency or legal assistance can tell you what applies to you. If you register in Oklahoma, there is a military rate.

  • Who: active-duty members who are Oklahoma residents or are stationed in Oklahoma, and their spouses.
  • How much: about $26 a year per vehicle.
  • How: fill out Form 779, the U.S. Armed Forces Affidavit, and have it certified by your unit or attach your orders. Take it to a tag agency with your title or current registration and proof of insurance.

It does not apply to retirees. Veterans rated 50% or higher have their own reduced registration.

Driver's licenses

You and your family members can keep driving on a valid out-of-state license while you are stationed in Oklahoma. If you want an Oklahoma license you need an Oklahoma address and a trip in person.

If you are an Oklahoma resident stationed somewhere else, your Oklahoma license does not expire while you are on active duty out of state.

A spouse's professional license

If your spouse is a nurse, a cosmetologist, or in another field licensed by a state board, Oklahoma has a fast lane. A military spouse with a license in good standing from another state can apply for an Oklahoma license, and the board has 30 days to issue it if the requirements are comparable. The application fee and the first license fee are waived. You can apply as soon as you have orders, before you move.

Property tax

Property tax is where being a resident matters.

  • The homestead exemption is small, about $108 a year in Lawton, and it requires Oklahoma residency. Active-duty members who keep another state as home do not get it.
  • The 100% disabled veteran exemption is large. A veteran with a 100% permanent service-connected rating who is an Oklahoma resident pays no property tax on their homestead. You file Form 998 with the county assessor.

I do not know of a property tax break just for being on active duty.

For veterans who stay

  • Sales tax. Veterans with a 100% permanent rating can get a sales tax exemption card.
  • Driver's license and tags. A free license for 100% disabled veterans, and a reduced registration for veterans rated 50% or more.
  • The VA funding fee. Any service-connected rating that pays compensation waives the funding fee on a VA home loan. That is federal, and it applies here like anywhere else.

The Oklahoma Department of Veterans Affairs has an office in Lawton and can walk you through what you qualify for.

Hunting and fishing

Active-duty members stationed in Oklahoma, and their families, buy hunting and fishing licenses at resident prices. With the Wichita Mountains and Lake Lawtonka close by, that one gets used.

Should you change your state of residence

This comes up, and it is not my question to answer. Changing your legal residence affects your taxes, your vote, your car, and more, and it has to be real. What I can say is that the pieces above are part of why some soldiers who finish their careers at Fort Sill decide to stay. Talk to legal assistance before you change anything.
