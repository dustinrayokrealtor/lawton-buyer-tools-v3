The rate

An O-1E without dependents at Fort Sill draws $1,230 a month in BAH in 2026. With dependents it is $1,605.

BAH is tax free and it follows the duty station. The published rate is the same for everyone at your grade and dependency status, whether they rent or buy.

If the rate drops next year you keep the higher number for as long as you stay at Fort Sill at the same grade and dependency status. That is called rate protection. New arrivals get the current year's rate. DoD usually publishes next year's rates in mid-December and they start January 1.

The rate is the Freddie Mac weekly average as of 10/8/26, and this number moves with it. Run your own numbers with BAS, disability pay, or a second income.

Property tax is figured at 1.11% of value a year and insurance at $19.28 per $1,000 of coverage. Those are Lawton numbers. A house you just bought can run a little higher on tax, closer to 1.2% inside Lawton city limits.

The median sale in Comanche County is $199,900 so far this year. That is about $690 a month short of the payment on a median-priced house. Every extra $100 a month you put toward the payment is roughly $10,400 more house, so base pay or a second income moves you up quickly.

If you have a service-connected disability rating of 10% or higher, the VA funding fee is waived and the same $1,230 carries about $130,000.

Adding BAS or base pay

BAS is $328.48 a month for officers in 2026. It is tax free and lenders count it. Put all of it toward the payment and the price goes to about $162,200.

I would not plan on spending all of it. BAS is your grocery money. What it tells you is that there is room above the BAH number if you want it. The BAH calculator lets you run it both ways, and the VA loan calculator adds base pay and a spouse's income to the picture.

Renting on the same allowance

A single-family house in Lawton rents for somewhere around $1,200 to $1,300 a month. This is a single-family-home town on both sides of the deal, so that is the number to compare against.

Your $1,230 lands right in that range. The same money carries about $128,000 if you buy, and the principal and interest stay fixed for thirty years while rent can go up every lease.

What decides it is usually how long you will be here and what you do with the house when you leave. Fort Sill turns over enough tenants that keeping it as a rental works more often than people expect.

Common questions

How much is O-1E BAH at Fort Sill in 2026?

$1,605 a month with dependents and $1,230 a month without. Those rates took effect January 1, 2026 for the Fort Sill / Lawton military housing area.

How much house can an O-1E without dependents buy at Fort Sill on BAH alone?

About $128,000 with a VA loan and nothing down at 7.40%, counting Lawton property tax and homeowners insurance. Base pay, BAS, or a second income raises that.

Can I use my Fort Sill BAH to qualify before I get there?

Yes. With orders in hand a lender can qualify you on the Fort Sill rate before you report. Plenty of families close within a week or two of arriving.
