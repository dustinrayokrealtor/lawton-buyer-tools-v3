What $150,000 buys in Lawton

A hundred and fifty thousand is where a lot of Fort Sill buyers shop, because it is close to what an enlisted allowance carries. Here is the payment, who it fits, and what to look at on a house at this price.

This price is under the county median of $199,900. That tells you two things. There is real inventory here, because roughly half of what sells in the county sells for less than the median. And a lot of it is not new.

What you are looking at for the money

I am not going to describe a typical house, because the list changes every week and a description would be out of date by the time you read it. What I can tell you is what the numbers say. The median house in Lawton was built in 1973. At a price under the median, expect a lot of what you see to be about that age or older.

With an older house, the condition of a few big items decides whether it is a good buy:

  • Roof. Ask its age first. It drives your insurance quote and it is something a VA appraiser looks at.
  • Heat and air. Ask the age of the system. Replacing one is a big expense you would rather know about now.
  • Paint. On a house built before 1978, peeling paint outside has to be fixed before a VA loan closes.
  • Foundation and drainage. Look at how water moves around the house.
  • Electrical and plumbing. A home inspector will tell you what is original and what has been updated.

Two houses at the same price can be very different buys depending on those five things. A house with a new roof and a newer air conditioner at $150,000 can be a better deal than a prettier one at $140,000 that needs both.

The cash it takes

  • Down payment: none on a VA loan.
  • Earnest money: a deposit with your offer. It comes back as a credit at closing.
  • Inspections and appraisal: a home inspection, a termite inspection, and $750 for the VA appraisal.
  • Closing costs: roughly 2% to 5% of the price, which is $3,000 to $7,500 here. A big piece of that is the first year of insurance. Sellers often pay some or all of it. The closing costs page goes line by line.

If you might keep it as a rental

Of the price ranges people shop here, this is the one where the payment lands closest to what a house rents for. A single-family house here rents for around $1,200 to $1,300 a month, so compare that to the payment above. Common questions

What is the monthly payment on a $150,000 house in Lawton?

About $1,441 a month with a VA loan and nothing down at 7.40%. That includes principal and interest, about $139 for property tax and about $241 for homeowners insurance at Lawton rates.

Can I buy a $150,000 house on BAH at Fort Sill?

Compare your BAH to a full payment of about $1,441. If your allowance is at or above that, BAH alone carries it. If it is under, the rest comes from base pay or a second income, and lenders count those too.

How much cash do I need to buy a $150,000 house with a VA loan?

There is no down payment. Plan on earnest money, a home inspection, a termite inspection and the $750 appraisal out of pocket early. Closing costs come on top, and sellers here often pay some or all of them.
