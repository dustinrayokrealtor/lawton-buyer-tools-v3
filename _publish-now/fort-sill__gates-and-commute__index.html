Fort Sill gates and the commute

Six gates, and they do not all keep the same hours. Here is which one serves which side of town, about how long the drive runs from each place people live, and what a visitor needs to get through.

6 gatesKey West, Key East, Scott, Bentley, 52nd Street and Apache
3 to 7 milesFrom most of central Lawton to the nearest gate
No vouchingEvery adult visitor without a DoD ID needs a pass

Rogers Lane, which is US-62, runs east and west along the north edge of Lawton. The post sits on the other side of it. Going west to east you pass 52nd Street Gate, Bentley Gate at Sheridan Road, Scott Gate at Fort Sill Boulevard, and then I-44. Key Gate is one exit north on the interstate. Apache Gate is on the far north side of post, off Highway 49.

Once you have that picture in your head, picking a side of town gets a lot easier.

Check the week you arrive, and check again when something feels off.

If somebody in the car does not have a DoD ID

This one catches people. Every adult without a military, dependent, or retiree ID needs a visitor pass to get on post, and every visitor in the vehicle gets checked. Fort Sill does not take part in the Trusted Traveler Program, so you cannot vouch your guests through on your own ID.

  • Passes come from the Visitor Welcome Center, Building 6701 on Sheridan Road, just south of Bentley Gate. A lot of people still call it the Visitor Control Center.
  • Bring a REAL ID or two valid forms of identification. There is a background check.
  • With a REAL ID you can request the pass online ahead of time. Do it at least 24 hours before you need it. Without one you go in person.
  • The center is not open around the clock. When it is closed, passes are issued at Bentley Gate.

If you are house hunting before you report, or you have family coming to help you move in, sort this out before anybody is sitting at the gate. Graduation and family day guests need passes too.

Common questions

Which Fort Sill gate is open 24 hours?

Scott Gate on Fort Sill Boulevard has been the most consistent 24-hour gate in 2026. Key Gate West off I-44 is normally 24 hours too, but it has had several long closures for barrier work since 2025. Check the My Army Post app or Fort Sill's gate page before you count on any of them.

What does a visitor need to get on Fort Sill?

Every adult without a DoD ID needs a visitor pass and a background check. Bring a REAL ID or two valid forms of identification. Passes come from the Visitor Welcome Center on Sheridan Road, just south of Bentley Gate, and REAL ID holders can request one online at least 24 hours ahead.

How long is the commute from Elgin or Cache to Fort Sill?

From Elgin it is roughly 12 minutes to Apache Gate when that gate is open and about 17 minutes to Key Gate. From Cache it is about 17 minutes to Bentley Gate. Those are map estimates with no traffic and no wait at the gate.
