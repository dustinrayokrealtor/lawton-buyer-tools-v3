Comanche County

Lawton property tax, explained

Property tax here is simple once you see the formula. Here is how the bill is figured, what it runs in each school district, and the exemptions worth knowing about.

The county does not tax you on the full value of the house. It taxes a slice of it, at a rate that depends on where the house sits.

The formula

There are three steps.

  1. Market value times 11.25%. That is the assessed value. Comanche County uses 11.25% for homes.
  2. Subtract exemptions. The homestead exemption takes $1,000 off the assessed value if you qualify.
  3. Multiply by the mill levy for your area. A mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of assessed value. The levy depends mostly on the school district.

Here it is on a $200,000 house inside Lawton, using the 2025 levy of 107.89 mills:

Market value$200,000
Assessed at 11.25%$22,500
Tax at 107.89 mills$2,428 a year
With the homestead exemption$2,320 a year
Per month in your paymentabout $200

Why your bill will not match the seller's

New owners should know this. Oklahoma limits how fast a home's taxable value can rise while the same person owns it. For a homestead the limit has been 3% a year. Somebody who has owned a house for fifteen years may be taxed on a value well under what it would sell for.

When the house sells, the cap comes off and the county can value it at what it is worth now, which is about what you paid. Your first full bill can be noticeably higher than the one the seller was paying. If you will not have a homestead exemption on file, ask the assessor which cap applies to you after that.

Two things follow from that.

  • Do not budget off the seller's tax bill. Budget off the formula above and your purchase price.
  • Expect your escrow payment to adjust after the first year. If the lender set it up from the old bill, it will come up short and the payment gets raised. Ask the lender to set it from the purchase price.

The calculators on this site use 1.11% of value, which is the median across all Lawton homes, long-held ones included. For a house you just bought inside Lawton, plan on something closer to 1.2%. On a $200,000 house the difference is about $15 a month.

Voters were asked in November 2026, in State Question 847, whether to lower those caps starting with 2027. The assessor's office can tell you which cap applies to the year you are buying in.

Exemptions

Homestead

It takes $1,000 off the assessed value, which is worth about $108 a year inside Lawton. You have to own the house and live in it on January 1, and file with the assessor by March 15. You file once.

The catch for military families: you have to be an Oklahoma resident. If you are active duty and keep another state as your legal residence, you do not qualify. It is a small number either way.

100% disabled veterans

This is the big one. A veteran with a 100% permanent service-connected disability pays no property tax on their homestead. The whole value is exempt. On a $200,000 house in Lawton that is about $200 a month back in your pocket, for as long as you own it and live in it. A surviving spouse keeps it.

  • You have to be an Oklahoma resident and own and live in the home on January 1.
  • You file Form 998 with the Comanche County Assessor. Bring the benefits letter from the Oklahoma Department of Veterans Affairs and your ID.
  • If you buy in the middle of the year, expect to owe tax for that first partial year unless you are moving the exemption from another Oklahoma home.

Seniors and lower incomes

There is a second homestead exemption for lower-income households, and a freeze on taxable value for owners 65 and older under an income limit. The assessor's office has the current limits.

When the bill comes

Statements are mailed in November for that calendar year. You can pay half by December 31 and the other half by March 31. If you have a mortgage with an escrow account, you never see the bill. The servicer pays it out of the money you send each month.

When you buy, the seller owes for the part of the year they owned the house. That gets handled as a credit to you at closing, and then you pay the whole bill when it comes out.

Who to call

The Comanche County Assessor's office is at 315 SW 5th Street in Lawton, Suite 301, 580-355-1052. They handle values and exemptions. The Treasurer, in the same building, handles the bills. They are the final word on anything on this page.

Common questions

What is the property tax rate in Lawton, Oklahoma?

For a house inside Lawton city limits in the Lawton school district, the 2025 levy worked out to about 1.2% of market value per year before exemptions. The median across all Lawton homes is lower, about 1.1%, because long-held homes are capped below market value.

When are property taxes due in Comanche County?

Bills go out in November. You can pay half by December 31 and the rest by March 31. If you have a mortgage with an escrow account, the servicer pays it for you.

Do disabled veterans pay property tax in Oklahoma?

A veteran with a 100% permanent service-connected disability pays no property tax on their homestead in Oklahoma. You have to be an Oklahoma resident, own and live in the home on January 1, and file Form 998 with the county assessor. A surviving spouse can keep the exemption.
